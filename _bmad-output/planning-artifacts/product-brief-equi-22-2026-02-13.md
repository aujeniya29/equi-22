## Executive Summary

A modern, high-performance website for an equestrian center in Yffiniac (Côtes-d'Armor, Brittany) designed to capture local search traffic and convert it into a steady stream of new riders. The center faces increasing competition and natural client turnover (teenagers outgrowing the sport) while relying on an aging website and word-of-mouth. With competitors poorly positioned online, there is a clear window of opportunity to establish local SEO leadership across the Saint-Brieuc and Lamballe catchment area. The project combines a static-site architecture with free/near-free hosting, a Google Business Profile-first strategy, and content that showcases the center's unique multi-discipline offering and caring pedagogy.

---

## Core Vision

### Problem Statement

The equestrian center in Yffiniac struggles to attract new clients through digital channels. Its current website is outdated, not mobile-friendly, and poorly optimized for search engines. Client acquisition relies primarily on word-of-mouth, pony rides at local events, roadside signage, and social media — channels that cannot scale to offset the natural turnover of teenage riders who leave the sport.

### Problem Impact

Without a steady flow of new riders — particularly children and adults — the center faces declining revenue. The problem is most acute during key seasonal peaks (school holidays, summer, back-to-school) when search demand spikes but the center remains invisible online. New competitors in the area compound the pressure, threatening to capture clients who search the web first.

### Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Competing equestrian centers in the Saint-Brieuc/Lamballe area have mediocre websites: poorly designed, not optimized for SEO, and lacking engaging content. None effectively leverages Google Business Profile, local schema markup, or service-specific landing pages. No competitor positions itself around multi-discipline riding or caring pedagogy — the digital landscape is wide open for a well-executed entry.

### Proposed Solution

A performant, mobile-first static website built with an SSG framework (Astro, Hugo, or 11ty) on free CDN hosting, paired with a Google Business Profile-first SEO strategy. The site will feature localized service×city pages, contextual pricing, a lightweight CMS for client-managed updates (news, pricing, schedules), and a hybrid navigation system combining service-based menus with visitor-profile routing. Priority zero: optimize Google Business Profile before the site even launches.

### Key Differentiators

- **Multi-discipline offering** — including Hunter and ethology, rare in the region and a powerful SEO net across multiple long-tail queries
- **Strategic location** — proximity to Saint-Brieuc (primary city) and Lamballe, covering the largest local catchment area
- **Caring pedagogy** — a welcoming, supportive approach that appeals to parents seeking a safe, rewarding experience for their children
- **Value positioning** — quality experience at reasonable pricing, with full transparency on rates
- **First-mover advantage** — competitors are digitally weak; seizing local SEO leadership now creates a durable competitive moat

## Target Users

### Primary Users

#### Persona 1: Sophie — Parent searching for riding lessons

- **Profile:** ~40 years old, 1–3 children, lives in or around Yffiniac/Saint-Brieuc area
- **Needs:** Year-round riding lessons, competition opportunities, holiday camps for her children
- **Search behavior:** Google on mobile or social media first, then verifies online (reviews, pricing, program details)
- **Decision drivers:** Google reviews, affordable pricing, clear program structure, reassuring photos of facilities
- **Fears:** Unclear pricing, unsafe environment, rigid or unwelcoming atmosphere
- **Success moment:** Sees transparent pricing, positive reviews, and a structured program — picks up the phone to book a trial lesson

#### Persona 2: Marc — Adult discovering or rediscovering riding

- **Profile:** ~45 years old, male or female, either rode as a teenager or complete beginner
- **Needs:** A relaxed, judgment-free environment to ride for pleasure — no competition pressure
- **Search behavior:** Google or social media discovery, often triggered by a life moment (new hobby, midlife activity)
- **Decision drivers:** Laid-back atmosphere, calm horses, friendly instructors, reassurance that "it's never too late"
- **Fears:** Feeling ridiculous, being too old, not fitting in with younger riders
- **Success moment:** Sees content showing adults of all levels having fun — feels reassured and makes contact

#### Persona 3: Claire — Horse owner seeking a boarding facility

- **Profile:** Experienced or semi-experienced rider, owns one or more horses, may have had them for years
- **Needs:** Affordable boarding with quality facilities, horse welfare focus, access to riding areas and trails
- **Decision drivers:** Price transparency, quality of installations (boxes, paddocks, arenas), horse well-being standards, surrounding environment for rides
- **Fears:** Poor care, hidden costs, inadequate facilities
- **Success moment:** Sees detailed facility photos, clear boarding pricing with inclusions, and evidence of horse welfare standards — calls to schedule a visit

### Secondary Users

#### The Center Manager (Content Admin)

- **Role:** Updates pricing, schedules, news, competition calendars, and seasonal programs via a lightweight CMS
- **Needs:** Simple, constrained editing interface that prevents content quality degradation — update content without touching code

#### The Developer (Aurélien)

- **Role:** Maintains site architecture, SEO optimization, and technical infrastructure
- **Needs:** Clean codebase, SSG workflow, Git-based content management

## Success Metrics

### User Success Metrics

- **Time to key information:** Visitors find pricing, schedules, and contact details within seconds of landing on any page
- **Contact friction:** Zero barriers between decision and action — sticky phone button and WhatsApp accessible from every page
- **Reassurance delivery:** Each persona encounters tailored reassurance content (safety for parents, no-judgment for adults, welfare proof for owners) on their first visit
- **Return visits:** Existing clients use the site regularly for schedules, news, competition calendars, and program updates

## MVP Scope

### Core Features

**Pages & Content:**
- Homepage with hybrid profile/service navigation ("I'm a parent / owner / rider" routing + classic service menu)
- Service pages: riding lessons (children), adult riding, horse boarding, holiday camps, competitions
- Pricing page (global) + contextual pricing on each service page
- About page: center presentation, instructor profiles (personality-focused), facilities
- Contact page with phone, WhatsApp, and contextual contact forms
- News/updates section
- Social media feed integration (Instagram/Facebook) for automatic living content

**Blog & SEO:**
- Blog technical structure ready for SEO content publishing
- 1–2 launch articles targeting primary keywords as proof of concept
- Localized service×city pages for primary catchment (Yffiniac, Saint-Brieuc)

**Local SEO Infrastructure:**
- Google Business Profile optimization (priority zero, pre-launch)
- LocalBusiness + Service schema markup on every page
- Directory registrations (Pages Jaunes, FFE, tourism offices)
- Meta tags, Open Graph, sitemap, robots.txt

**Technical Foundation:**
- SSG framework (Astro, Hugo, or 11ty) with free CDN hosting
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Performance budget: < 2s load, Lighthouse 90+ on all metrics
- Images in WebP/AVIF, lazy loading, responsive srcset
- Sticky phone button + floating WhatsApp on every page

**UX & Conversion:**
- Hybrid navigation: service-based menu + homepage visitor-profile routing
- Reassurance content integrated into every service page
- Clear CTAs on every page driving phone/WhatsApp contact
- Immediate notification to center manager on form submissions
